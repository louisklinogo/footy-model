## 4) APPLY HANDICAP (CRITICAL STEP)

## 5) EVALUATE OUTCOME

For each adjusted score:

* If Away + handicap ≥ Home → win/draw

## 6) COMPUTE PROBABILITY

Sum probabilities:

> P(Away +2 succeeds) = Σ P(i, j) where (j + 2 ≥ i)

Repeat for:

* +1
* +2
* +3
