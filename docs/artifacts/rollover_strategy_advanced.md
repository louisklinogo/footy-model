# The "Smart Rollover" Strategy: Daily Action Plan

**Objective:** Start with **50 GHS** and aim for **1,000,000 GHS**, but secure your cash along the way so you never lose everything.

## The 3 Golden Rules
1.  **Rule 1 (The Free Hit):** Withdrawal your **50 GHS** stake immediately when you hit **100 GHS**. You are now betting with "House Money".
2.  **Rule 2 (The Salary):** After every win (once risk-free), withdraw **10% of the profit** to your MoMo wallet. Do not re-bet this.
3.  **Rule 3 (The Lock):** When you hit a milestone (**500, 5,000, 50,000**), withdraw **50% of the total balance** immediately.

## How to Execute (The Steps)

1.  **Fund your account:** Desposit exactly **50 GHS**.
2.  **Stick to ONE Strategy:** Don't mix 1.5 odds today and 2.0 odds tomorrow. It messes up the math.
3.  **Withdraw Immediately:** When the Daily Action says "Withdraw", do it **immediately** after the bet settles. Do not let it sit in the account. Move it to MoMo.
4.  **The "Bust" Scenario:** If you lose on Day 4, look at your "MoMo Banked" column. You likely still have your 50 GHS stake + profit secured. You can restart without pain.
