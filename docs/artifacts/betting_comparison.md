## 4. Deep Dive: Niche Markets (Corners, Fouls, Obscure Leagues)

If you are a specialist bettor looking for edge in **stats markets** (corners, cards, fouls) or **obscure leagues**, the platforms differ significantly:

### **1xBet: The Specialist's Choice (Winner)**
*   **Stats Coverage:** Unmatched. You can bet on **exact foul counts**, **player cards**, **first foul**, **offside totals**, and **shots on target** for many leagues.
*   **League Depth:** Covers the widest range of "obscure" leagues (e.g., lower divisions in Asia/South America, youth leagues).
*   **The Edge:** If you track referee stats or team foul averages, 1xBet is the only one giving you direct markets to exploit that data.

### **Betway: The Alternative**
*   **Stats Coverage:** Good, but different. Uses a **"Booking Points"** system (10pts Yellow, 25pts Red) rather than raw card counts for some markets. "Build a Bet" allows combining corner/card stats effectively.
*   **League Depth:** Solid, but tends to focus more on established professional leagues.

### **Msport: The Generalist**
*   **Stats Coverage:** Limited mainly to **Corners** (Total, Handicap, First/Last). Specific markets for "Fouls" or individual player cards are rarely available or harder to find.
*   **League Depth:** Good for general betting, but less depth for truly obscure semi-pro leagues compared to 1xBet.
